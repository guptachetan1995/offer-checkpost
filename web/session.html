Offer Checkpost

Open the address printed in the terminal

This browser is not signed in as the person. Open the newest address printed in the terminal where Offer Checkpost was started. It looks like http://127.0.0.1:8741/?token=…, with the port you chose.

That address is what makes a browser the person, the only one who publishes or retracts a verdict, records an outcome, or spends searches after the evidence is decisive. Anything that talks to the app without it, a script, an MCP client or another browser, acts as the agent.

Each address works once: when it is opened, the terminal prints the next one, which makes another browser the person instead. An address already opened, or one from an earlier run, opens nothing.
